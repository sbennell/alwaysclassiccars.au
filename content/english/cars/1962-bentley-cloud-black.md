  content: "This 1962 Bentley Silver Cloud SII (chassis B 110 DW) has a remarkable story that spans Tasmania, prestigious ownership, and the silver screen. Originally acquired by Dockers 3611, Charles's most notable chapter came when he was owned by the late George Forbes, President of the Rolls Royce Club. Imagine the prestige - the President of the Rolls Royce Club himself chose this Bentley as his personal vehicle, a testament to Charles's quality and character. The car migrated from Tasmania to Melbourne, crossing the Bass Strait to begin a new life on the mainland. In 1999, Charles became a member of the Always Classic Cars wedding family, but his adventures were far from over. In 2011, Charles achieved Hollywood fame when he was featured in 'The Eye of the Storm,' a major Australian film starring Academy Award nominees Judy Davis and Geoffrey Rush. Picture this elegant Bentley on the silver screen, driven by these legendary actors, captured in cinematic glory. Charles's media career continued in 2017 when REVIEW Magazine selected him for a fashion model shoot along with other promotional work - his classic black livery and timeless elegance making him the perfect backdrop for high-fashion photography. Powered by a 6.23-litre 8-cylinder engine running on 95-octane fuel, Charles combines Rolls Royce Club prestige, film credentials, and fashion industry cachet in one extraordinary Bentley."
