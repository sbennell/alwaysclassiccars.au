  title: "'Oxford' - Where the Rolls Royce <br> Legacy Began"
  content: "This 1975 Rolls Royce Shadow holds the most significant place in Always Classic Cars history - Oxford is THE vehicle that started everything. Tom Roberts worked as a driver for Astra Taxis. Together with his wife Josie Roberts, they founded Always Classic Cars with a beautiful vision: to provide a gift for weddings and service to brides. Initially, they started with brands like Ford and Jaguar, but in early 1987, Tom and Josie made a pivotal decision that would define their company's future - they shifted their loyalty to Rolls Royce. Oxford was their first. The story of Oxford's acquisition is remarkable. The vehicle belonged to Tim White (the same Tim White who later sold ACC the 1970 Shadow that became Pixie Queen), but Tim faced financial challenges. AGC Finance had the vehicle repossessed and held it to cover expenses. Oxford arrived at Always Classic Cars in pieces - not a running vehicle, but a disassembled restoration project. But this turned out to be a blessing in disguise. ACC had an array of contacts in the automotive industry, and they knew where to locate most of the parts required to bring this Shadow back to life. Piece by piece, part by part, Oxford was restored. Through the AGC Finance deal, something even more valuable happened: the introduction of Mr. Aldo Verrocchi, who became one of ACC's longest-serving chauffeurs and remains with the team today. Powered by a 6.7-litre 8-cylinder engine running on 95-octane fuel, Oxford represents the founding moment when Tom and Josie Roberts committed to Rolls Royce excellence. Every Rolls Royce in our fleet today exists because of Oxford."
