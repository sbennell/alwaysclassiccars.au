  content: "One of the most sought-after ultra-luxury cars of all time, the Silver Wraith was manufactured in limited numbers between 1946 and 1959, making Shelly truly rare. Completed in August 1948 (chassis WCB69, engine W197B), this H. J. Mulliner touring limousine in blue with opera roof light (body 4958) was delivered via the prestigious Kellow-Falkiner dealership to John Carter of Werribee, Victoria in April 1949 (registered VIC-221) - who cherished it for over a decade. Through the 1970s-82, she belonged to Malvin D. Cooper of Victoria (registered LEH-741), then passed to Peter W. Shellard of Victoria in 1982 (registered RR-613). In the 1990s, Shelly embarked on an international adventure to Japan. Upon Peter Shellard's passing, Always Classic Cars acquired this magnificent vehicle in 2005. Named 'Shelly' in his honor, this automobile was favored by heads of state worldwide and became one of our first vehicles classified as a Limousine."
