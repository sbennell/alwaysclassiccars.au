  title: "'Bertha' - A Story of <br> Restoration & Rebirth"
  content: "From corporate Sydney to countryside Victoria and back again, 'Bertha' has witnessed decades of Australian history. Originally delivered in Australia and owned by Tall Strop Insurance in Sydney, this magnificent Silver Wraith was once exhibited in the prestigious Kellow Falknier's showroom. Through the 1960s, she resided with Dr. E. J. C. Hamp in Drouin, Victoria (bearing registration HLT-972), before returning to NSW in the 1980s with Graeme Soderland. The 1990s saw Bertha with Garrath Will and later Michael Bristow of Armadale, who worked for Silver Lady Car Hire. When Always Classic Cars acquired her in December 1991, Bertha was in extremely poor condition - the undercarriage, upholstery, and wiring all needed extensive work. Through painstaking restoration, this post-war classic was brought back to her former glory, and now creates unforgettable wedding memories."
