This magnificent Phantom V (Engine 01920, Chassis SCAZS DXBCH01920, English Registration 8888WF) was initially owned by the prestigious Westin Lennox Hotel in Atlanta, Georgia, where it served as the courtesy car for the stars of the day. Imagine: FRANK SINATRA, DEAN MARTIN, DORIS DAY, and other entertainment legends who performed live shows at the hotel all traveled in this very limousine. When the hotel faced financial difficulties in the early 1980s and went into liquidation, Princess Sebrina was delivered on March 27, 1981, to Armstrong-Massey in North Humberside, England. Her ownership from 1984 to 1992 remains unrecorded - a mysterious period adding to her intrigue. Prior to Always Classic Cars' acquisition, Princess Sebrina belonged to a Mr. Miles (surname uncertain) and was used for chauffeuring Sydney's elite, including the famous racehorse trainer Gai Waterhouse. In 2002, this Phantom V with the legendary Rat Pack heritage joined the Always Classic Cars family, bringing Atlanta glamour, English elegance, and Sydney sophistication together in one extraordinary vehicle."
