  content: "From the 1928 Amsterdam Olympics to your wedding day, 'Lord Burghley' has witnessed nearly a century of history. Built in 1935 for Cecil David George Brownlow, Lord Burghley, 6th Marquess of Exeter - the Olympic Gold Medalist whose thrilling 400-meter hurdles victory was immortalized in the Academy Award-winning film 'Chariots of Fire.' This magnificent Sedanca De Ville then passed to an English pilot, crossed the Atlantic to Max Wigington of South Carolina in 1967 (bearing Antique Plate #770), was discovered by world yachtsman George Ronald Lovett during a four-year sailing tour in 1992, and finally arrived at Always Classic Cars in March 2000. Despite very little mileage, this remarkable vehicle has traveled three continents and graced the lives of extraordinary people."
