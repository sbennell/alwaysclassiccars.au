  title: "'PINA' - Actual Royal Family <br> Limousine"
  content: "This is no replica - PINA is an authentic Royal limousine that carried British Royalty. Built in the early 1950s and associated with the Royal Family for social events, this Vanden Plas-bodied Austin Princess (Model A120) has an extraordinary documented history. In 1953, the late Prince Philip used PINA during a Royal Tour of Australia. When Queen Elizabeth II did not attend the 1956 Olympic Games opening in Melbourne, Prince Philip used PINA again for this historic occasion. The limousine was then kept at the Albert Park Depot for future Royal Tours. In 1975, PINA was transported to Papua New Guinea for Queen Elizabeth II's use in the Independence Day Parade. Later returned to Melbourne (likely due to government corruption in PNG), this Royal treasure was accidentally discovered undercover in extremely poor condition - but still drivable - at a tire place in North Melbourne. Vince Mennilli offered to take and restore it to its original status. Always Classic Cars acquired this historically significant vehicle in 2008 and registered it as 'PINA' in honor of Mrs. Josepina Roberts, one of the founders of ACC."
