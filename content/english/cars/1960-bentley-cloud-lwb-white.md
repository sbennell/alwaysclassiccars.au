  content: "Among the rarest Bentleys in existence, this 1960 Silver Cloud II Long-Wheel-Base is one of only 57 ever built. Powered by the Rolls Royce-Bentley L Series V8 engine, Betsie's extraordinary journey began in the palaces of North Africa as the personal vehicle of King Hassan II of Morocco. From Moroccan royalty, she made her way to South Africa, where a car dealership in Pretoria used her for promotional work. The family who owned her in South Africa knew her affectionately as 'Big Betha.' When this family migrated to Perth, Australia, they brought Big Betha to our shores - a remarkable story discovered when the son of the South African owner visited our showroom and shared this incredible history. She was later owned by Tony Zammit before Always Classic Cars acquired her in 2009. Renamed 'Betsie,' she joined the ACC family, bringing three continents of royal and promotional heritage to Australian wedding celebrations."
