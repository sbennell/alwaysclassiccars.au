In 1995, ACC acquired this 1970 Rolls Royce Shadow sedan from Tim White. But this Shadow had a destiny beyond being a regular sedan - ACC envisioned her as a custom stretch limousine to meet the continuing demand. There was a specialist company in Hawthorn that stretched vehicles, and Pixie Queen was sent there for her transformation. Between 1995 and 1996, the custom stretching process took place. This wasn't simple work - the historical records note that 'numerous challenges' were overcome during this period. Imagine the complexity: cutting a Rolls Royce in half, extending the chassis, ensuring structural integrity, maintaining the luxury interior standards, preserving the classic Rolls Royce quality throughout the modification. Every challenge was methodically solved. By early 1997, after all the obstacles had been conquered, Pixie Queen was successfully completed and joined the Always Classic Cars fleet. Powered by a 6.7-litre 8-cylinder engine running on 95-octane fuel, seating 7 passengers in extended luxury, Pixie Queen represents ACC's commitment to meeting client needs - when demand required larger vehicles, they didn't just buy one, they custom built one."
