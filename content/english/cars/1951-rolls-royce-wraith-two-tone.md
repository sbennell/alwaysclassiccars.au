  title: "'Lady Di' - A Princess's Legacy <br> Lives On"
  content: "When Diana, Princess of Wales owned and loved her 1951 Rolls Royce Wraith, Always Classic Cars knew they had to honor her memory. In 1996, they acquired this matching Silver Wraith and named it 'Lady Di.' This Park Ward saloon in green (chassis WHD57, engine W56D, body N119) came off test in April 1950 and was shipped in February 1951 via the prestigious Kellow-Falkiner dealership to Vere LeR. Stokoe of Olympia Motors, Melbourne, who took possession on May 1, 1951. Registered as VIC UP-000 and exhibited from August to September 1951, Lady Di remained with the Stokoe family until July 1997 - an extraordinary 46 years with one family. She then passed to May Nichols of the renowned ASPRO Family before spending time abandoned at McDermott's Workshop. David Palmer rescued her and sold her to the late Tom Roberts, who lovingly restored this Silver Wraith to her former glory. Now Lady Di continues Princess Diana's legacy, bringing royal elegance to Australian weddings."
